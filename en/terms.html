Using SlowMovie - Guitar Picking Cam

Last updated: October 12, 2026

SlowMovie - Guitar Picking Cam and SlowMovie Picking Cam for Mac (together, "the app") are for checking movement in slow motion, your own or that of people who have agreed to be filmed, when practicing an instrument, a sport and the like. Please read the following before you use it.

  1. Filming requires consent. Use the app only where the people being filmed have agreed to it. You must not use the app to film anyone without their consent, or for any filming that is against the law.
  2. It is not a tool for hidden filming. A device used as the camera works only with the app in the foreground and its screen on. The screen always shows the name of the device that is watching and that recording is in progress. Do not cover or tamper with the device in order to hide this.
  3. You are responsible for what you do with your videos. Get the consent of anyone who appears in a video before you publish or share it.
  4. Mind copyright. If you publish a video of a performance, take care over the rights in the music.
