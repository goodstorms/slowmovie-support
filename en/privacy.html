SlowMovie - Picking Cam Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

SlowMovie - Picking Cam and SlowMovie Picking Cam for Mac (together, "the app") are provided by Takamichi Teraoka ("the developer"). The app lets you check movement, such as playing an instrument, in slow motion. This page explains what information the app handles.

Information the developer collects

None. The app does not send your personal information, the video or sound you record, or any usage data to the developer or to any third party's servers. There is no account to create. The app contains no advertising or analytics.

Information handled on your devices

The app handles the following only on your own devices.

  • Video and sound: What the camera device records is sent, encrypted, directly to the controller device you have approved on the same Wi-Fi network. It does not travel over the internet or through any server of the developer. Once a clip has been transferred, it is not kept on the camera device.
  • Saved videos: Stored on the controller device in the place you chose (your photo library, or a folder you choose).
  • Pairing information: The names of the devices you have approved and the keys used to encrypt the connection are stored in the device's keychain. You can remove them at any time from the camera's screen.
  • Settings: Your chosen role, save location and similar preferences are stored on the device.

Device features the app uses

  • Camera and microphone: Used on the camera device to record video and sound.
  • Local network: Used to find and connect to your other device on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Photo library: Used to add videos when it is the save location (the default on iPhone and iPad) (add-only; the app does not read your existing photos or videos).
  • Microphone (Mac app): Used when you choose to record sound from an audio interface connected to your Mac.

Purchases

In-app purchases and the trial are recorded by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name or payment details.

Respect for people being filmed

The app is meant to be used where the people being filmed have agreed to it. The camera device's screen always shows the name of the device that is watching and that recording is in progress.
