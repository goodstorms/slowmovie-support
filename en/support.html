SlowMovie - Picking Cam Support

About

SlowMovie - Picking Cam (on the Mac: SlowMovie Picking Cam Remote; called SlowMovie below, as it is inside the app) turns an iPhone or iPad into a camera that you control from a second device (an iPhone, iPad or Mac) to make slow-motion videos. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Videos

See how it works, from setting up to recording and saving the slow-motion video.

Frequently asked questions

The camera does not appear in the list.

  • On the camera device, is the app in the foreground with Use as Camera chosen?
  • Are both devices on the same Wi-Fi network (the same network name)? Guest networks often block devices from talking to each other.
  • On both devices, is Local Network turned on in Settings → SlowMovie?

A number appears the first time I connect.
Check that both screens show the same six-digit number, then tap Allow on the camera. Only devices you have allowed can control the camera. You will not be asked again.

It says the versions are different.
Update the app on both the camera device and the controller.

How long does the trial last?
Seven days from the moment you tap Start the 7-Day Trial. Reinstalling the app does not extend it. The days left are shown under the camera list and the Record button.

I bought the full version but the limits are still there.
Tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

I bought it on iPhone. Can I use it on my Mac?
The iPhone and iPad app and the Mac app are separate apps and separate purchases. iPhone and iPad are covered by one purchase on the same Apple Account.

Does a device that is only the camera need the purchase?
No. Only the controller (the device that receives the videos) needs it.

What is the best way to set the focus?
Let autofocus find it first, then switch to manual.

  1. In the capture settings, set Focus to Auto and let it get the focus roughly right.
  2. Switch to Manual. The focus stays where autofocus had it, and is locked there.
  3. Fine-tune it with the Focus Distance slider.

Left on Auto, the focus keeps moving as your picking hand moves, which makes the movement harder to see. Locked in Manual, it stays put, and your picking is easier to follow.

Can I start and stop recording with my hands full?
On Mac, the space bar starts and stops recording (pressed during the countdown, it cancels). With a foot pedal that can be set to send the space key, as many USB and Bluetooth page-turner pedals can, you can do it with your foot while you play.

How can I check that sound is coming from my audio interface?
On Mac, choose Mac Audio as the audio source: an input level meter appears below L ch and R ch. The bars grow as you play. Yellow is loud and red is too loud. If CLIP lights up in red, the sound is clipping: turn the input down on the interface.

On Mac, the sound from my audio interface doesn't come through.
Please check the following.

  1. In the Audio settings, Audio Source is set to Mac Audio.
  2. Microphone access: in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone, the app is turned on. After turning it on, quit and reopen the app.
  3. Device and channels: choose your interface under Device, and the input your guitar is plugged into under L ch and R ch (for input 2, choose 2). When you play, the level meter below should move.

Can I see the clip as it was shot, without waiting for the conversion?
On Mac, turn off Keep Pitch in Capture Settings. Clips are then saved just as they arrive from the iPhone, without slow-motion conversion, and open in your default player, such as QuickTime Player (full version; dual-camera clips are still converted).

Can I use it without Wi-Fi?
The devices need to be on the same network. A Personal Hotspot or a travel router works.

Practising takes up more and more storage with every take.
Turn on Practice Mode (Don’t Save) at the top of the Save To settings. Takes are then converted and played back but not saved. Each new take replaces the last one, and it is gone when you quit the app, so storage doesn’t grow however many takes you shoot. To keep a take you like, use Save This Video below the Record button; it goes to the destination in the settings.

Where are my videos saved?
On iPhone and iPad: in your photo library, or in a folder you choose (your choice in the settings; the folder can be opened in the Files app). On Mac: in the folder you choose (you choose it before your first recording, and can change it later with Change under Save To in the settings). All of these are outside the app, so your videos stay if you delete it (except when, on iPhone or iPad, you choose the SlowMovie folder in the Files app as the save folder: the videos in that folder are deleted together with the app).
