SlowMovie - Picking Cam Support

About

SlowMovie - Picking Cam (on the Mac: SlowMovie Picking Cam for Mac; called SlowMovie below, as it is inside the app) turns an iPhone or iPad into a camera that you control from a second device (an iPhone, iPad or Mac) to make slow-motion videos. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Frequently asked questions

The camera does not appear in the list.

  • On the camera device, is the app in the foreground with Use as Camera chosen?
  • Are both devices on the same Wi-Fi network (the same network name)? Guest networks often block devices from talking to each other.
  • On both devices, is Local Network turned on in Settings → SlowMovie?

A number appears the first time I connect.
Check that both screens show the same six-digit number, then tap Allow on the camera. Only devices you have allowed can control the camera. You will not be asked again.

It says the versions are different.
Update the app on both the camera device and the controller.

How long does the trial last?
Seven days from the moment you tap Start the 7-Day Trial. Reinstalling the app does not extend it. The days left are shown under the camera list and the Record button.

I bought the full version but the limits are still there.
Tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

I bought it on iPhone. Can I use it on my Mac?
The iPhone and iPad app and the Mac app are separate apps and separate purchases. iPhone and iPad are covered by one purchase on the same Apple Account.

Does a device that is only the camera need the purchase?
No. Only the controller (the device that receives the videos) needs it.

What is the best way to set the focus?
Let autofocus find it first, then switch to manual.

  1. In the capture settings, set Focus to Auto and let it get the focus roughly right.
  2. Switch to Manual. The focus stays where autofocus had it, and is locked there.
  3. Fine-tune it with the Focus slider.

Left on Auto, the focus keeps moving as your picking hand moves, which makes the movement harder to see. Locked in Manual, it stays put, and your picking is easier to follow.

Can I use it without Wi-Fi?
The devices need to be on the same network. A Personal Hotspot or a travel router works.

Where are my videos saved?
On iPhone and iPad: in your photo library, or in a folder you choose (your choice in the settings; the folder can be opened in the Files app). On Mac: in the SlowMovie folder inside Movies, or a folder you choose. All of these are outside the app, so your videos stay if you delete it.
